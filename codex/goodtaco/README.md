# GoodTaco for ChatGPT and Codex

GoodTaco turns your AI into an app builder for your business. Describe the tool your team needs in plain words, like a work order tracker for your crew, an inventory list or a job schedule, and it builds it on GoodTaco: it sets up the app's database, writes the screens and logic, opens the app to check it works, and puts it on a private preview link. When you like it, ask it to publish the app and your team uses it from any phone or computer.

It can also use the Google Sheets, Gmail and other services you've connected in GoodTaco, read an app's logs to fix problems, and go back to an earlier version. GoodTaco hosts and runs the apps; the building happens on your own AI plan.

You sign in on GoodTaco's own page and pick the workspace it may use. Nothing is published unless you ask. It works on GoodTaco apps only: it can't delete apps or workspaces, connect outside accounts for you (you do that in GoodTaco), or reach apps in workspaces you didn't choose.

## Install in Codex

```
codex plugin marketplace add deplyr-corp/goodtaco
codex plugin add goodtaco@goodtaco
```

## What you can ask for

- "Build a work order tracker for my crew and give me the preview link."
- "Show me my GoodTaco apps and what each one does."
- "Add a priority field to my work order tracker."

## Signing in

The first time you use it, GoodTaco asks you to sign in once. Sign in, pick your workspace and click **Connect**. You
never paste a password or key into the chat. You can remove access any time from your GoodTaco settings at
https://console.goodtaco.ai.
